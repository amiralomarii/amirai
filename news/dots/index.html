The shift, in brief

Personal AI is moving toward ongoing assistance between conversations.

OpenAI introduced Dots on September 29 as a direction toward ongoing personal assistance. The important idea is continuity: remembering context, working across connected tools and making progress between interactions, subject to the product’s access and permission settings.

IN FOCUSAI that works when you are not in the chat

Always-on personal agents bring persistence, connected apps and ongoing tasks into the product conversation. The story below separates the announcement from its broader implications and limitations.

EXPLAINED SIMPLY

What does that actually mean?

Instead of opening an app each time, imagine a tool that can remember a project, monitor relevant developments and bring back progress.

01 / THE STORY

What actually happened

OpenAI describes Dots as persistent agents that can work on goals over time. The product introduces connected apps and a cloud computer for carrying out work.

OpenAI describes Dots as agents with their own cloud computer, lasting context and connections to external applications. Their promise depends on working reliably beyond a single chat turn.

02 / THE STORY

Why this matters

Most assistants still wait for a person to ask for each step. An ongoing agent changes the relationship: the software maintains context and may make progress while the user is away. That could be useful for recurring research and administration.

Users should be able to see which apps are connected, inspect activity and control what the agent may do autonomously. Persistent access creates a different privacy and security profile from single-use prompts.

WHY IT MATTERS

This convenience raises useful questions about permissions, data retention, reliability and how easily users can stop background activity.

03 / THE STORY

What to be careful about

Always-on does not mean unlimited authority. Access, geographic rollout and supported connections can vary, and users should understand approval controls before linking sensitive accounts.

04 / THE STORY

What to watch next

Watch how these agents present activity histories, handle changing goals and request authorization before important actions. The quality of oversight may matter as much as task capability.

A mature experience needs understandable activity logs, dependable pause controls and explicit authority boundaries, especially for spending or sharing information.

IN PRACTICE / WHAT TO WATCH

The practical consequence

A persistent agent may help track developments in a long-running project, prepare material between discussions or notice when connected information changes. These benefits depend on clear authority and sensible notification behavior.

The important controls are the quiet ones: reviewing connected access, understanding what the agent remembers and withdrawing a permission when a task is finished. Users need to remain in charge.

A question worth asking
